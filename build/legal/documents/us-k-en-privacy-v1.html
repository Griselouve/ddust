Your secret information (for adventurers)

Application: Donjons & Savons
Region: United States
Audience: Players under 18
Version: 1 — 2 August 2026

This page explains what information the game keeps about you, and how it is protected. If you have any questions, ask an adult in your clan. The rules of the game are on another page.

1. What the game knows about you

Almost nothing! The game only keeps:

  • your nickname and your avatar,
  • your experience, your level, your hit points and your items,
  • the fact that you are a child (but not your date of birth: the game forgets it straight away),
  • your clan's adventures (the chores defeated, the clan journal).

2. What the game does NOT know

  • The photos you take stay on the phone. They are never sent to the Internet, and nobody else can see them — not even your parents in the game.
  • The game knows neither your real name, nor your address, nor your school. (So do not write them in your nickname!)
  • There is no advertising, and nobody spies on you to show you any. Nobody buys or sells anything about you.

3. Who can see your nickname?

Only the members of your clan. No other clan can see who you are or what you do, and there is no list of players anywhere. There is no chat: you cannot talk to anyone you do not know in the game.

4. Your clan's story

When the clan's chest is opened, the game can write a lovely story telling your adventures. To write it, it sends your clan's journal — with the players' nicknames, and nothing else — to a computer that writes the text. Then an adult in your clan decides whether to share that story, or keep it for yourselves.

5. Your parents watch over you

It is an adult responsible for you who gave permission for the game to keep your information, by accepting the rules and bringing you into their clan. They can look at it and make it disappear whenever they want, and they can also tell the game to stop keeping anything about you at all.

When they brought you in, the game reminded them that they are declaring they are your parent or guardian. They are the one who answers for what they declared: the game cannot check it on its own. Their own rules are in the grown-ups' privacy policy.

6. Where your information is kept

Right at the start, the game asks you to choose a region of the world: that is where your information is kept, and it never moves from there. Here it is the United States. Each region has its own rules, because the laws are not the same everywhere. And because your information never travels, everyone in your clan must have chosen the same region as you.

7. Making your information disappear

If you or your parents no longer want to play, an adult can delete your account. This can be done in the game, or on the page dvddust.web.app/delete-account.

Here is what happens, in order:

  • Straight away: you disappear from the game. Your character, your nickname and your adventures are no longer visible anywhere, to anyone.
  • For 30 days: your information is put away in a closed chest, outside the game. Nobody uses it. It is just in case someone pressed the button by mistake.
  • After that: it is erased for good, like magic. We only keep a note of the day your parents said yes, and of the day they said stop — so we can prove it if we are ever asked.

Careful: this is final. If you play again later, you will start over from scratch, with a new character.

8. Any questions?

An adult in your clan can write to donjons@grisloup.com.
