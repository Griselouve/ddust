Privacy Policy

1. Data controller

The controller for the processing of data of the "Donjons & Savons" application is grisloup.com. For any question about your data or to exercise your rights, write to donjons@grisloup.com.

2. Data we collect

Donjons & Savons is designed around the principle of minimisation: the application only collects what is strictly necessary for it to work.

DataDetails
Account identifierThe technical identifier of your Google account (we store neither your name nor your email address in the game data).
RegionThe storage region selected (today: European Union).
Legal statusThe single distinction minor / adult. Your date of birth is never kept: it is only used, at first sign-in, to compute that status, and is then forgotten.

Above all, what the application does not collect:

  • Chore photos never leave the phone: they are saved in the application's private space on your device. Only a technical identifier is sent to our servers to indicate that a proof exists; the image itself is never uploaded or shown to anyone.
  • No analytics: Google Analytics collection is disabled.
  • No advertising identifier (AD_ID) and no advertising, in accordance with the Google Play Families policy.
  • No geolocation, no contacts, no content from your other applications.

4. Minors and parental consent

Use of the application by a minor is subject to the consent of their legal guardian. That consent is given by the adult when they accept the Terms of Service — section 6 of which sets out that undertaking precisely — and it is exercised when that adult invites the minor into their clan, accepts their membership or creates a player profile for them. Acceptance of the terms is time-stamped and kept.

A minor cannot create a clan or join the game on their own: entry into a clan happens only by invitation from an adult, by QR code or by an encrypted link protected by a confidential code. The age thresholds applicable in the European Union are 13 and 18. A version of this policy written in accessible language is shown to minor players.

5. Processors

  • Google Cloud / Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd): hosting, database, authentication and notifications. Processing is governed by the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum, which we have accepted.
  • Google Play: distribution of the application.
  • Google Gemini API (Vertex AI): generation of narrative content. Three uses, each triggered by an explicit action of a user: suggesting a clan name and description, suggesting a task name and description, and writing the clan's adventure story offered for sharing after a loot chest has been opened. For that last use, the clan name, its description and the clan's event log — which contains the nicknames chosen by the players — are transmitted to the model. No other personal data is sent: no account identifier, no date of birth, no photo, no individual statistic. Processing is carried out on Google infrastructure located in the European Union, and the data transmitted is not used to train the models.

6. Sharing carried out by the user

The application offers two kinds of sharing, always at the initiative of an adult of the clan and through the sharing applications on your device:

  • an invitation to join the clan (link or QR code);
  • the clan's adventure story, offered after a loot chest has been opened. That text contains the clan name and the nicknames of its members.

Once shared, this content is beyond our control and is governed by the terms of the destination service (messaging app, social network, etc.). We encourage you to bear this in mind before sharing, particularly where the content mentions children.

7. Location and security

Your data is stored in the region you selected (for the European Union: data centres located in the EU). Exchanges between the application and our servers are encrypted. Access to data is restricted by security rules that isolate each clan: a clan cannot view another clan's data, and there is no directory allowing players or clans to be enumerated.

8. Retention periods

  • Game data: kept for the life of the account, deleted when the account or the clan is deleted.
  • Evidence of terms acceptance: kept as long as the account exists, deleted with it.
  • Chore photos: they stay on your device and are deleted along with the application.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over your data, as well as over the data of minors for whom you are the legal guardian. To exercise them:

  • Immediate deletion: from the application, or via the page dvddust.web.app/delete-account (sign in with your Google account; permanent deletion of all your data).
  • Any other request: write to donjons@grisloup.com. We reply within one month at most.

Parental consent cannot be withdrawn retroactively, but you can at any time delete the minor's data by deleting their account or the clan.

10. Changes

This policy may change, in particular to support new features. Any new version is published at the same address and announced in the application. The version date appears at the top of the document.

11. Complaints

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your country of residence — in France, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
