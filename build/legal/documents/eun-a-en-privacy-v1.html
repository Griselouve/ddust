4. Minors and parental consent

Use of the application by a minor is subject to the consent of their legal guardian. That consent is given by the adult when they accept the Terms of Service — section 6 of which sets out that undertaking precisely — and it is exercised when that adult invites the minor into their clan, accepts their membership or creates a player profile for them. Acceptance of the terms is time-stamped and kept.

At the very moment of that act — inviting a member, creating a player profile, accepting a membership request — the application reminds the adult that they are declaring they are the minor's legal guardian, or that they are acting with that guardian's agreement when the minor already belongs to another clan, and that they consent to the collection of their data, and gives them access to the terms of service. That declaration is their responsibility alone: the application performs no verification of the guardianship relationship.

A minor cannot create a clan or join the game on their own: entry into a clan happens only by invitation from an adult, by QR code or by an encrypted link protected by a confidential code. The threshold is 18: any player under 18 is subject to their legal guardian's consent, without exception. The application is therefore stricter than Article 8 of the GDPR, which lets each Member State — and, through the European Economic Area Agreement, Norway — set between 13 and 16 the age of a minor's own digital consent (13 in Denmark, Finland, Norway and Sweden): we never rely on that option. A version of this policy written in accessible language is shown to minor players.

5. Processors

  • Google Cloud / Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd): hosting, database, authentication and notifications. Processing is governed by the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum, which we have accepted.
  • Google Play: distribution of the application.
  • Google Gemini API (Vertex AI): generation of narrative content. The content produced is meant to be read by children: the model's safety filters are set to the strictest level the interface offers, for the four categories of harmful content (harassment, hate speech, sexually explicit content, dangerous content), and that setting is imposed by our servers and not by the device. The instructions given to the model further bound the register: no violence towards any person or animal, nothing frightening, no adult themes, no coarse language. Four uses, each triggered by an explicit action of a user: suggesting a character name and description, suggesting a clan name and description, suggesting a task name and description, and writing the clan's adventure story offered for sharing after a loot chest has been opened. For that last use, the clan name, its description and the clan's event log — which contains the nicknames chosen by the players — are transmitted to the model. No other personal data is sent: no account identifier, no date of birth, no photo, no individual statistic. Processing is carried out on Google infrastructure located in the European Union, and the data transmitted is not used to train the models.

6. Sharing carried out by the user

The application offers three kinds of sharing, always at the initiative of an adult of the clan and through the sharing applications on your device:

  • an invitation to join the clan (link or QR code);
  • the clan's activity log, or the adventure story drawn from it after a loot chest has been opened. That text contains the clan name, the nicknames of its members and the tasks they have completed.
  • a gift code from the shop, which contains no personal data.

Once shared, this content is beyond our control and is governed by the terms of the destination service (messaging app, social network, etc.). We encourage you to bear this in mind before sharing, particularly where the content mentions children.

On first use, you select a storage region. Your data is stored in that region, and in that region only (for Northern Europe: data centres located in the EU; as Norway belongs to the European Economic Area, this storage involves no transfer outside the EEA). This choice cannot be changed and data cannot be migrated from one region to another: if the application is reinstalled, a region will have to be selected again and data associated with another selection will not be transferred.

Each region has its own terms of service and its own privacy policy, adapted to the law applicable to it. This policy is the one for the Northern Europe region. The list of open regions and the full set of their documents is available at donjons.grisloup.com/en/legal.

Exchanges between the application and our servers are encrypted. Access to data is restricted by security rules that isolate each clan: a clan cannot view another clan's data, and there is no directory allowing players or clans to be enumerated.

8. Retention periods

Deleting an account happens in three stages: immediate functional deletion (account unusable, profile removed from the clan, data no longer exposed in the game), then a limited retention period outside the service, then permanent erasure.

  • Game data: kept for the life of the account. On deletion it leaves the service immediately, then is kept for 30 days under restricted access — solely to handle a mistake, a support request or fraud, and for no other purpose — before permanent erasure or anonymisation.
  • Evidence of terms acceptance: kept for 5 years from the withdrawal of consent or the deletion of the account. This period matches the general limitation period under French law (article 2224 of the Civil Code) and rests on the legal obligation to demonstrate that valid consent — in particular the parental consent of Article 8 GDPR — was actually obtained. This evidence is kept closed and deactivated: it is no longer used to operate the service.
  • Data of a clan whose subscription has stopped: kept two years from the clan being locked, then permanently deleted. This period is deliberately long: what is costly to rebuild is not the clan but its history — the chores settled, the levels reached, everyone's journal — and a family that stops has not always given up. One month before the deadline, we warn the adults of the clan.
  • Chore photos: they stay on the player's device and are deleted as soon as the chore is judged — at the latest the next time the application is opened, when the verdict was given while it was closed. Deleting your account from the application also erases any that remain.

Beta waiting list: deleted in full at the end of the beta phase, or earlier on request (section 12).

9. Your rights, and withdrawing your consent

Under the GDPR, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over your data, as well as over the data of minors for whom you are the legal guardian. To exercise them:

  • Deletion: from the application, or via the page donjons.grisloup.com/delete-account (sign in with your Google account). It follows the schedule set out in section 8.
  • Any other request: write to donjons@grisloup.com. We reply within one month at most.

Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time — both your own and the consent you gave as legal guardian for a minor you let play.

For a child you make play, withdrawal is exercised from the application, in their character management menu: the option “Withdraw my consent and erase this child's data” is offered to the chiefs of their clan of origin. It takes effect immediately — the child no longer plays and their data is no longer exposed in the game — then, after a 3-day reflection period during which a chief of the clan of origin may reverse the decision, their game data is deleted, then kept for 30 days under the conditions of article 8 before its definitive erasure.

For your own consent, withdrawal is exercised by deleting your account, from the application or via donjons.grisloup.com/delete-account.

Withdrawing your consent ends the processing of the child's data. It does not prevent another responsible adult from one day inviting them into another clan, under their own responsibility: we keep no list of excluded children, because it would mean holding information indefinitely about a child whose complete erasure we have just been asked for. A child, however, can never enter alone — it always takes the invitation of an adult who declares that they answer for them.

For a minor, deleting the clan chief's account dissolves that clan and, by cascade, the profiles of the minors whose clan of origin it was, as well as any profile that no longer belongs to an active clan — including children without their own account, whose game data exists only within the clan. The withdrawal then takes effect for them as it does for you.

Withdrawal only applies going forward: in accordance with Article 7(3) GDPR, it does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it. That is precisely why the evidence of the consent obtained, and of the date on which it was withdrawn, is kept under the conditions of section 8.

If you would rather not go through account deletion, write to donjons@grisloup.com: we will handle your request within one month.
