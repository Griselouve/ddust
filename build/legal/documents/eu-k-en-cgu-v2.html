The rules of the game (for adventurers)

Application: Donjons & Savons
Region: European Union
Audience: Players under 18
Version: 2 — 1 August 2026

Welcome, adventurer! Before you enter the dungeon, here are the important rules, explained simply. If anything is not clear, ask an adult in your clan.

1. What is this game?

Donjons & Savons turns the chores of the house into monsters to defeat. By really helping your family, you earn experience, levels and rewards chosen by your parents. Your family forms your "clan".

2. Your parents said yes for you

So that you can play, an adult who is responsible for you (a parent or your guardian) accepted the rules of the game and brought you into their clan. They are the one who decides which chores you can do and who checks your work. If you join another clan later on, it will always be by invitation from an adult.

3. Safety first

The monsters are imaginary, but the chores are real! Only do the chores your parents have given you, and always ask an adult for help with anything that could be dangerous: cleaning products, electrical appliances, heavy objects or things up high. No experience point is worth an injury.

4. Your account and your nickname

You sign in with a Google account. Your nickname and your clan's name can only be seen by the members of your clan: no other clan can see who you are, or what you do. There is no messaging, no chat, and you cannot meet anyone you do not know in the game.

Choose a kind nickname — no bad words, no meanness. And do not put your surname, your address or the name of your school in it: keep that to yourself.

5. Your information is protected

The game only keeps what it needs for you to play: your nickname, your avatar, your experience. It does not know your date of birth. The photos you take to prove that a chore is finished never leave the phone. There is no advertising at all in the game.

Only one thing leaves the game, and only if an adult in your clan decides so: when the clan's chest is opened, the game can write the story of your clan's adventures so it can be shared. That story contains the players' nicknames — never anything else. It is always an adult who chooses whether to share it, or not.

6. There is nothing to buy

The game is free and there is nothing you can buy in it. Your rewards come from your parents, not from a shop. If a window ever asks you for money or for a code, call an adult straight away.

7. When you grow up

When you come of age, an adult in the clan will be able to "declare you an adult" in the game. You will then have to accept the grown-ups' rules yourself in order to keep playing.

8. If the rules change

If these rules change, the game will show them to you again and you will have to accept them to continue the adventure.

Any questions? An adult in your clan can write to donjons@grisloup.com. Have a great adventure!
