Your secret information (for adventurers)

Application: Donjons & Savons
Region: European Union
Audience: Players under 18
Version: 1 — 1 August 2026

This page explains what information the game keeps about you, and how it is protected. If you have any questions, ask an adult in your clan.

1. What the game knows about you

Almost nothing! The game only keeps:

  • your nickname and your avatar,
  • your experience, your level, your hit points and your items,
  • the fact that you are a child (but not your date of birth: the game forgets it straight away),
  • your clan's adventures (the chores defeated, the clan journal).

2. What the game does NOT know

  • The photos you take stay on the phone. They are never sent to the Internet, and nobody else can see them — not even your parents in the game.
  • The game knows neither your real name, nor your address, nor your school. (So do not write them in your nickname!)
  • There is no advertising, and nobody spies on you to show you any.

3. Who can see your nickname?

Only the members of your clan. No other clan can see who you are or what you do, and there is no list of players anywhere. There is no chat: you cannot talk to anyone you do not know in the game.

4. Your clan's story

When the clan's chest is opened, the game can write a lovely story telling your adventures. To write it, it sends your clan's journal — with the players' nicknames, and nothing else — to a computer that writes the text. Then an adult in your clan decides whether to share that story, or keep it for yourselves.

5. Your parents watch over you

It is an adult responsible for you who gave permission for the game to keep your information, by accepting the rules and bringing you into their clan. They can look at it and make it disappear whenever they want.

6. Making your information disappear

If you or your parents no longer want to play, an adult can delete your account: all your information is then erased for good, like magic. This can be done in the game, or on the page dvddust.web.app/delete-account.

7. Any questions?

An adult in your clan can write to donjons@grisloup.com.
