The rules of the game (for adventurers)

Application: Donjons & Savons
Region: Oceania
Audience: Players under 18
Version: 1 — 28 August 2026

Welcome, adventurer! Before you enter the dungeon, here are the important rules, explained simply. If anything is not clear, ask an adult in your clan.

1. What is this game?

Donjons & Savons turns the chores of the house into monsters to defeat. By really helping your family, you earn experience, levels and rewards chosen by your parents. Your family forms your "clan".

2. Your parents said yes for you

So that you can play, an adult who is responsible for you (a parent or your guardian) accepted the rules of the game and brought you into their clan. They are the one who decides which chores you can do and who checks your work. If you join another clan later on, it will always be by invitation from an adult — but the grown-up who answers for you is still the one from your first clan.

When they brought you in, the game reminded them that they are declaring they are your parent or guardian and that they agree to the game keeping your information. They are the one who answers for what they declared. They can also look at your information whenever they want, and ask for it to be erased. Their own rules are here: the grown-ups' terms of service — you can read them together if you want to know everything.

3. Safety first

The monsters are imaginary, but the chores are real! Only do the chores your parents have given you, and always ask an adult for help with anything that could be dangerous: cleaning products, electrical appliances, heavy objects or things up high. No experience point is worth an injury.

4. Your account and your nickname

You sign in with a Google account. Your nickname and your clan's name can only be seen by the members of your clan: no other clan can see who you are, or what you do. There is no messaging, no chat, and you cannot meet anyone you do not know in the game.

Choose a kind nickname — no bad words, no meanness. And do not put your surname, your address or the name of your school in it: keep that to yourself.

5. Your information is protected

The game only keeps what it needs for you to play: your nickname, your avatar, your experience. It does not know your date of birth. The photos you take to prove that a chore is finished never leave the phone. There is no advertising at all in the game, and nobody buys or sells anything about you.

Only one thing leaves the game, and only if an adult in your clan decides so: when the clan's chest is opened, the game can write the story of your clan's adventures so it can be shared. That story contains the players' nicknames — never anything else. It is always an adult who chooses whether to share it, or not.

Everything is explained in detail in Your secret information.

6. An adult pays, never you

The game is paid for by the family: an adult in your clan pays a subscription, and the whole clan benefits from it. There is also a shop, with worlds, backdrops and avatars to buy — but only the adults of your clan can buy anything there.

You never have to pay. You cannot buy anything, and the game will never ask you for money or for a card number. If a window asks you for money or for a code, something is wrong: call an adult straight away.

And above all: nothing that can be bought makes you stronger or faster. You cannot pay for levels — those are earned by defeating real monsters. As for your rewards, they come from your parents, not from a shop.

And what if one day the adult who pays cannot manage it any more? Don't worry: the game does not stop all at once. Your clan keeps going for several weeks, and it is not for you to sort out — that is a grown-up matter. If nobody starts the subscription again, your clan eventually goes to sleep, and its information is erased after three months. It is never your fault, and you lose nothing of what you really did.

7. The place where your information is kept

Right at the start, the game asks you to choose a region of the world: that is where your information is kept. Here it is Oceania. Each region has its own rules of the game, because the laws are not the same everywhere. Your rules are the ones on this page. And because your information stays in its region, everyone in your clan must have chosen the same one as you.

8. When you grow up

When you come of age, an adult in the clan will be able to "declare you an adult" in the game. You will then have to accept the grown-ups' rules yourself in order to keep playing.

9. If the rules change

If these rules change, the game will show them to you again and you will have to accept them to continue the adventure. Today's rules are always at this address: oceanie-k-en-cgu-v1.html.

Any questions? An adult in your clan can write to donjons@grisloup.com. Have a great adventure!
