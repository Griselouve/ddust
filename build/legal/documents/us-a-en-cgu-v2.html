2. Purpose and description of the service

Donjons & Savons is a family game that turns household chores into a role-playing adventure: the members of a single family form a "clan", chores are represented as monsters, and completing them earns experience points, levels and rewards defined by the adults of the clan (the "loot"). The Application is a playful family-organisation tool; it does not provide any childcare, educational or supervision service.

Access to the Application is based on a subscription, preceded by a 15-day free trial. A shop also offers content available for one-off purchase. The Application contains no advertising.

  • Free trial — 15 days, with no restriction on features. At the end of the trial the subscription automatically takes over, unless cancelled before it expires.
  • Subscription — it is taken out by the clan chief and covers every member of their clan: one subscription and one payer per clan. It renews automatically until cancelled.
  • Shop — additional content (worlds, themes, sets of chores) and cosmetic items (avatars, backdrops) can be bought individually and permanently. These purchases are optional: they confer no progression advantage and do not condition the use of the Application.
  • Reserved for adults — both subscribing and purchasing are reserved for the adults of the clan. A minor cannot subscribe, buy, or enter any payment method.
  • Billing — the subscription and purchases are made through and billed by the download platform (Google Play), not directly by the publisher. Current prices are displayed by the platform before any payment.
  • Cancellation — it is done from the subscription settings of your Google account, and never from the Application: the publisher has no control over billing. It takes effect at the end of the current period, the remainder of which is not refunded.
  • No active subscription — at the end of the trial or after cancellation, access to the clan's features is suspended. Your data and your clan's data are kept and become accessible again if you resubscribe; they are only deleted by deleting the account (section 11).

5. Clans, roles and invitations

The first adult who creates a clan becomes its chief. Clan chiefs have administration functions: managing and configuring tasks, validating completed work, filling and distributing the loot, inviting and removing members, and promoting other adults to the chief role. These functions are reserved for adults.

A clan can only be joined by invitation from a clan chief: by scanning a QR code, or through an encrypted invitation link accompanied by a confidential code communicated by word of mouth. There is no clan directory, no search, and no way to discover a clan you have not been invited to. Clans are sealed off from one another: no clan can view another clan's data.

A clan exists in a single storage region: its members must all have selected the same region. An invitation issued in another region cannot be accepted, and the Application will say so plainly.

The clan name has an internal version (chosen by its members, visible only within the clan) and an external version generated by the Application. You agree not to use names or nicknames that are abusive, discriminatory or unlawful, and not to include personal data in them (surname, address, school, etc.).
