4. Children under 13 — COPPA

Donjons & Savons is a family application meant to be used by children under the supervision of the adults of their clan. The information we collect from a child is the same as for any player, and no more: a nickname, an avatar, game progression, and the fact that the player is a minor. We do not collect a child's real name, date of birth, address, email, phone number, photograph or location, and the app has no messaging, no chat and no public profile.

How parental consent is obtained. A child cannot enter the game on their own: there is no child sign-up, no clan directory and no search. A child only ever enters a clan through an invitation issued by an adult of that clan, or through a player profile that adult creates for them. The adult accepts the Terms of Service — section 6 of which sets out that undertaking precisely — and, at the very moment of the act (inviting a member, creating a player profile, accepting a membership request), the application restates that they are declaring themselves the child's parent or legal guardian, or that they are acting with that parent or guardian's agreement when the child already belongs to another clan, and that they consent to the collection of that child's game data. Acceptance is time-stamped and kept. Consent therefore always precedes collection.

That declaration is the adult's responsibility alone: the application performs no verification of the parent-child relationship (section 3 of the Terms of Service).

Your rights as a parent. At any time you may:

  • review the personal information we have collected from your child — it is visible in the application, on the child's character sheet and in the clan's activity log, and we will provide it on request;
  • have it deleted — from the application, from dvddust.web.app/delete-account, or by writing to us;
  • refuse any further collection or use of it — which means the child stops playing, since their game data has no existence separate from the game.

Write to donjons@grisloup.com to exercise any of these. We never condition a child's participation in the game on disclosing more information than is reasonably necessary to play, and we do not disclose a child's information to third parties other than the service providers listed in section 5, which act on our instructions.

The age thresholds applicable in the United States are 13 and 18. A version of this policy written in accessible language is shown to minor players.

If you believe we have collected information from a child without the consent of their parent or guardian, write to donjons@grisloup.com: we will delete it.

5. Service providers

  • Google Cloud / Firebase (Google LLC): hosting, database, authentication and notifications. Processing is governed by the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum, which we have accepted.
  • Google Play: distribution of the application.
  • Google Gemini API (Vertex AI): generation of narrative content. Three uses, each triggered by an explicit action of a user: suggesting a clan name and description, suggesting a task name and description, and writing the clan's adventure story offered for sharing after a loot chest has been opened. For that last use, the clan name, its description and the clan's event log — which contains the nicknames chosen by the players — are transmitted to the model. No other personal information is sent: no account identifier, no date of birth, no photo, no individual statistic. Processing is carried out on Google infrastructure located in the United States, and the data transmitted is not used to train the models.

These providers act on our instructions and for no other purpose. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising — neither yours nor your child's. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months, and we have no plans to.

6. Sharing carried out by the user

The application offers two kinds of sharing, always at the initiative of an adult of the clan and through the sharing applications on your device:

  • an invitation to join the clan (link or QR code);
  • the clan's adventure story, offered after a loot chest has been opened. That text contains the clan name and the nicknames of its members.

Once shared, this content is beyond our control and is governed by the terms of the destination service (messaging app, social network, etc.). We encourage you to bear this in mind before sharing, particularly where the content mentions children.

7. Location, regions and security

On first use, you select a storage region. Your data is stored in that region, and in that region only (for this region: data centres located in the United States). This choice cannot be changed and data cannot be migrated from one region to another: if the application is reinstalled, a region will have to be selected again and data associated with another selection will not be transferred.

Each region has its own terms of service and its own privacy policy, adapted to the law applicable to it. This policy is the one for the United States region. The list of open regions and the full set of their documents is available at dvddust.web.app/en/legal.

Exchanges between the application and our servers are encrypted. Access to data is restricted by security rules that isolate each clan: a clan cannot view another clan's data, and there is no directory allowing players or clans to be enumerated.

8. Retention periods

Deleting an account happens in three stages: immediate functional deletion (account unusable, profile removed from the clan, data no longer exposed in the game), then a limited retention period outside the service, then permanent erasure.

  • Game data: kept for the life of the account. On deletion it leaves the service immediately, then is kept for 30 days under restricted access — solely to handle a mistake, a support request or fraud, and for no other purpose — before permanent erasure or anonymisation.
  • Evidence of terms acceptance: kept for 5 years from the withdrawal of consent or the deletion of the account. This record exists so that we can demonstrate that valid consent — in particular the parental consent required by COPPA — was actually obtained, and on what date it was withdrawn. It is kept closed and deactivated: it is no longer used to operate the service.
  • Chore photos: they stay on your device and are deleted along with the application.

9. Your rights, and withdrawing your consent

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to obtain a copy of it, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Several state laws — including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and comparable laws in Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states — provide such rights. We extend them to every user of this region, whichever state you live in, and to the information of children for whom you are the parent or legal guardian.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is no opt-out to exercise on that count. We collect no sensitive personal information within the meaning of those laws.

  • Deletion: from the application, or via the page dvddust.web.app/delete-account (sign in with your Google account). It follows the schedule set out in section 8.
  • Any other request — access, a copy, correction, or a question: write to donjons@grisloup.com. We reply within 45 days at most, and will tell you if we need longer. You may use an authorised agent; we will ask for proof of that authorisation.

To protect you, we verify a request by requiring that it come from, or be confirmed by, the Google account tied to the data. If we cannot verify it, we will say so rather than act on it.

Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time — both your own and the consent you gave as a parent or legal guardian for a child you let play.

Withdrawal is exercised by deleting your account, from the application or via dvddust.web.app/delete-account. There is no other form of withdrawal: the game data we hold has no existence separate from the account it belongs to.

For a child, deleting the clan chief's account dissolves that clan and, by cascade, the profiles of the children whose clan of origin it was, as well as any profile that no longer belongs to an active clan — including children without their own account, whose game data exists only within the clan. The withdrawal then takes effect for them as it does for you.

Withdrawal only applies going forward: it does not make unlawful what was collected while the consent was valid. That is precisely why the evidence of the consent obtained, and of the date on which it was withdrawn, is kept under the conditions of section 8.

If you would rather not go through account deletion, write to donjons@grisloup.com: we will handle your request.
