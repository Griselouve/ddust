The roadmap

Where is the dungeon heading? Here are the milestones!

  • December 2026

    Launch

    The complete game loop: family clan, monster-chores, parent validation, levels, the loot chest opening ceremony and the clan journal. In French, English, Spanish, Italian, German, European Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese and Dutch.

  • Early 2027

    More content

    Donjons & Savons is coming to Apple! And new reasons to come back: weekly challenges to take on as a clan, merits to earn…

  • 2027

    The loot pack!

    The monsters will drop gold! Items to equip, potions with mischievous effects, quest scrolls and small real-life favours.

  • 2028

    Character classes

    Every hero will pick a path: the thief skims extra gold off quick chores, the bard boosts the whole clan whenever they get to work, and certain classes together unlock synergies. Enough to give everyone a real role in the family.

  • To be continued…

    Theme packs

    Replay the whole adventure in another universe: Pirates, Mafia, Space Opera… To bring the magic back once the dungeon has no secrets left.
