The roadmap

Where is the dungeon heading? Here are the milestones. The distant dates are bearings, not promises carved in stone: the community will shape what comes next.

  • Autumn 2026

    Launch

    The complete game loop: family clan, monster-chores, parent validation, levels, the loot chest opening ceremony and the clan journal. In French, English and Spanish, with a 14-day trial and then a single subscription for the whole clan.

  • Early 2027

    More content

    New reasons to come back: weekly challenges to take on as a clan, merits to earn, and bestiaries and collectible items to complete along the way.

  • 2027

    The loot & favours pack

    The monsters will drop gold! A heroes' shop, items to equip, potions with mischievous effects, quest scrolls and small real-life favours (“ten extra minutes of screen time”, “the right to pick dessert”).

  • 2027

    Character classes

    Every hero will pick a path: the thief skims extra gold off quick chores, the bard boosts the whole clan whenever they get to work, and certain classes together unlock synergies. Enough to give everyone a real role in the family.

  • To be continued…

    Theme packs

    Replay the whole adventure in another universe: Space Station, Academy of Magic, Wild West… To bring the magic back once the dungeon has no secrets left.
