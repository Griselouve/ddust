The concept

Everyone knows the chore wars. Here's how we win them — together.

The problem (you already know it)

Getting kids to pitch in around the house means repeating, negotiating, sometimes shouting. Magnetic chore charts end up forgotten on the fridge after two weeks. One thing was missing: making tidying your room genuinely exciting.

How the game works

Tasks are monsters

Every household task is represented by a monster, with its own difficulty level and its own resting time before it comes back. The child picks their target, completes the task for real, declares “I defeated the monster!” — then a parent validates the work.

Experience and levels

Every victory advances the player and the whole clan. Levels bring titles to collect — who hasn't dreamed of being “Dirty Sock Sniffer”? And an experience nudge lets parents get a straggler moving again.

The clan is the family

The household forms a clan with its own name, crest and adventure log. Two gauges rise with every victory: the clan's level, and the chest. When the chest is full, it's time to share it out.

The loot is very real

The chest is filled by the parents: pocket money, a family outing, pizza night… On opening, the promised money is shared in proportion to each player's experience, and the items are drawn at random in favour of those who have received the least.

Two roles, one game

Parents, clan chiefs

They choose and configure the tasks (suited to each age), validate the work, fill the loot chest and run the game from an admin screen. They can play themselves — or not. A single notification is enough to validate a task in one tap.

Kids, heroes of the dungeon

They create their character, choose their avatar from a gallery of heroes, set off on the hunt and see their efforts rewarded. The youngest can play without a phone: a parent creates a profile for them and plays on their behalf from their own.

Built for trust

  • No ads, no trackers — no analytics, no advertising ID.
  • Photos never leave the phone — a photo of a completed task stays local, it is never sent to our servers.
  • Every clan is sealed off — no other clan can see your data. There is no directory, no search and no messaging: you only join a clan when an adult invites you.
  • No encounters with strangers — no chat, no outside players, no content sent by third parties.
  • Date of birth is never stored — only the child/adult distinction is kept.
  • You never pay to progress — a family subscription after a 14-day trial, plus a shop for content and backdrops. Never a currency to top up, never a progression advantage for sale, and only the adults of the clan can buy.

The details are in our legal and privacy space.

Donjons & Savons is designed and developed by a parent and independent developer (grisloup.com), first for his own family — then for yours.
