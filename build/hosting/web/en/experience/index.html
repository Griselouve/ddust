Enter the dungeon

Here is what a family experiences, from the first evening to the opening of the chest. Follow the guide — the tour begins at the tavern.

Chapter 1

One evening, at the tavern

It all begins like any good adventure: with a tavern door pushed open. The guild's crest — crossed feather duster and broom — welcomes you. The situation is clear: your house has become a dungeon infested with dusty monsters. Heroes are needed.

The player answers the call of adventure, introduces themselves and dives into the adventure. The tone is set from the very first minute: you're not installing a chore app, you're setting out on a quest.

Chapter 2

The birth of a clan

A parent founds the clan — its name, its destiny. Each member then picks their avatar from a gallery of colourful heroes. Grizzled ranger dad, elf archer daughter, the littlest one a knight in armour: the family portrait suddenly takes on a whole new look.

Children cannot come in on their own: you only join a clan on an adult's invitation, by scanning their QR code or, remotely, with a link paired with a code read out loud. The game calls that sending the invitation “by carrier pigeon”.

Chapter 3

Charging the household monsters

The hunt screen presents the dungeon room by room: the bathroom with its zombie in the bathtub, the kitchen in flames, the laundry basket sticking its tongue out, the Cerberus of the pets' corner… The child picks their monster and heads into battle — in real life. A photo can serve as proof; it stays on the phone, nothing on the network.

Back home, they announce “I defeated the monster!”. A parent rules: victory, half-victory or failure — in one tap, straight from the notification. If it's a win, the fanfare sounds, the experience drops in, the bar goes up, and the trash really has been taken out.

Chapter 4

The clan's chest

While the kids do battle, the parents stock the chest: a little promised pocket money, a secret note, the promise of an outing. The clan is told that a deposit has been made — but its contents stay a mystery until the very end.

When the chest gauge is full, a chief summons the clan: “Gather together, if possible for real, in the same room”. Everyone confirms they are present from their own device, and when the last one has answered, the gold pours out. Each hero then discovers their share in private: the money in proportion to their exploits, the items drawn at random in favour of those served least. And the hero who contributed the least gets special treatment — a kiss attack from the whole clan, announced to everybody… except them.

Chapter 5

Clan life

The clan screen tells the rest of the story: the levels of some, the hit points of others — because stay too long without a fight and it's the grime that gets the better of you — and the two gauges climbing towards the next chest. The notes the chiefs had slipped in are finally revealed, and the clan log keeps a trace of every exploit, day after day.

With every chest opened, the clan comes away with the tale of its adventure: a text that parents can share, or keep to themselves. It's the dashboard of a family playing together — and incidentally, of a house that has never been so tidy.
