Legal space

The documents that govern Donjons & Savons — in plain words, and in your language. Every document exists in an adult version and a child version (written in accessible language), and every country has its own.

Your country

On first use, Donjons & Savons asks you which country you live in. That choice decides which terms of service and which privacy policy apply to you, under the law of that country. The app does not geolocate you: your declaration is what counts.

This choice is final. It also decides which servers host your data, and that data cannot be moved afterwards. If the app is reinstalled, the country will have to be chosen again, and data linked to another choice will not be transferred.

The documents of the country you chose are the ones that bind you. They are also available at any time from within the app.

Delete my account

Deletion is final: you will not be able to recover your data. It takes effect immediately (account unusable, profile removed from the clan), then the data is kept for 30 days outside the service before permanent erasure. If the account owns the clan, the clan is deleted and the profiles of the minors who no longer have a legal guardian follow.

To withdraw your consent for a child you let play, there is no need to delete your account: in the app, on the clan screen, open the child's menu and choose “Withdraw my consent and erase this child's data” (an option reserved for the chiefs of their clan of origin). The child leaves the game at once; for 3 days, “Restore this child” cancels the decision. After that, the child's game data is deleted in all their clans on the same schedule, and their account is deactivated (if they have no account, their profile is erased).

Cancel your subscription before deleting your account, from the Google Play Store: deletion does not cancel it and it would keep being billed. Content bought in the shop is lost along with the account.
