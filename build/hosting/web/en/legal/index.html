Legal space

The documents that govern Donjons & Savons — in plain words, and in your language. Every document exists in an adult version and a child version (written in accessible language), and every region has its own.

Your region

On first use, Donjons & Savons asks you to choose a storage region. Your data is hosted in that region, and in that region only.

This choice is final. Data cannot be migrated from one region to another. If the app is reinstalled, a region will have to be selected again, and data associated with another selection will not be transferred.

Every region has its own terms of service and its own privacy policy, adapted to the law applicable to it. The documents of the region you chose are the ones that bind you — they are also available at any time from within the app.

Delete my account

Deletion is final: you will not be able to recover your data. It takes effect immediately (account unusable, profile removed from the clan), then the data is kept for 30 days outside the service before permanent erasure. If the account is the clan's owner account, then its clan is deleted and the profiles of the minors who no longer have a legal guardian follow.

An adult who wishes to withdraw their consent for a child they let play goes through this same deletion, on this page: the child's account is deleted.

Cancel your subscription before deleting your account, from the Google Play Store: deletion does not cancel it and it would keep being billed. Content bought in the shop is lost along with the account.

Go to the deletion page →

Cookies

This site is cookie-free! No analytics, advertising or tracking cookie is ever set, on any page.

The only exception: the account-deletion page uses Google Sign-in (Google Identity Services), which may set its own technical cookies, strictly necessary for authentication and managed directly by Google.
