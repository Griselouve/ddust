Your kids won't tidy their room?
Turn your home into a dungeon.

Donjons & Savons is a family game that turns chores into monsters to defeat. Every task completed — for real — earns experience, levels and a loot chest that parents fill with real rewards.

Kids get to work

Taking out the trash becomes “escorting the Pestilent Cart”. Cleaning the toilet, “defeating the Ceramic Hydra”. The urge to level up does the rest — no shouting, no bribery.

Parents stay in charge

You choose the tasks on offer, validate the work done and fill the loot chest: pocket money, a family outing, a little treat… You decide everything.

The family plays together

Your household forms a clan. Everyone's experience makes the whole group grow, and when the chest is full, you gather to open it — with a little ritual — like a true end of quest.

An adventure that begins at home

The household has turned into a vast dungeon full of treasures and dusty monsters. The valiant parents can't keep up: so the “Corvée Nostra” guild sends in its finest team of adventurers — the kids.

Designed by a parent and independent developer, with no ads and no needless data collection: a game made for families, not for advertisers.
