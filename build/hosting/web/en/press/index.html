The pitch

In one sentence

“Your kids won't tidy their room? Donjons & Savons turns your home into a role-playing dungeon — and chores into monsters to defeat.”

In one paragraph

“Donjons & Savons is an Android game that turns family chores into an adventure: the family forms a clan, every household task becomes a monster, and every victory — validated by a parent — earns experience for the player and the clan alike. When the clan's chest is full, the family gathers to open it: inside, the very real rewards the parents slipped into it. Developed by a parent and independent developer, with no ads, no trackers and no needless data collection.”

The story behind the game

Donjons & Savons was born from a parent's observation: at home, there's no shortage of motivation — it's just entirely invested in video games. Rather than fight the dragon, why not enlist it: the game applies to chores the very recipes that make RPGs irresistible (progression, loot, team quests), while keeping parents in control and the rewards in real life. The project is developed solo, alongside a family life that serves as a permanent testing lab.
